ORCHID PURCHASE MANAGER PROFESSIONAL 4.9.94
===============================================

Decoration-service cleanup
--------------------------
- Shopify product 750B (Higher Stitch Count Embroidered Left Chest Logo) is
  recognized as an Orchid decoration charge, not a garment.
- Decoration-only charges are excluded from Product Master, Purchase Review,
  vendor purchase orders, and outsourced-decoration reports.
- Existing saved decoration-charge rows are removed from Product Master on
  upgrade. Orchid saves a dated Product Master backup before that cleanup.
- Normal garments that happen to use embroidery continue through the standard
  purchasing workflow unchanged.
