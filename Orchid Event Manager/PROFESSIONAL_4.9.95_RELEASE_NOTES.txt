ORCHID PURCHASE MANAGER PROFESSIONAL 4.9.95
===============================================

Product Master cleanup
----------------------
- EMBARK, American Flag, and Embark 2 Logo Embroidery are permanently
  recognized as Shopify decoration-service charges.
- Product 800 is recognized as Orchid in-house bulk stock. It remains in the
  Shopify sale but is never a Product Master, Purchase Review, or vendor PO
  item.
- Existing saved copies of those items are removed automatically when Product
  Master opens. Orchid saves a dated Product Master backup first.
- Bare color/size labels such as Medium Black are rejected as phantom product
  records and cannot return through a later import.
- The same exclusions apply during import, Product Master synchronization,
  Purchase Review, final purchase-order generation, and catalog cleanup.

Existing boot purchasing, A64CQ color aliases, Tru-Spec purchase-as style
numbers, and purchase-order folder opening behavior are unchanged.
