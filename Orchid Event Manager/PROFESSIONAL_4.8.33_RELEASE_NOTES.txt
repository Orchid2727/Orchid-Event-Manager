ORCHID PURCHASE MANAGER PROFESSIONAL 4.8.33

CUSTOMER-INSTRUCTION TARGETING, REQUIRED DECORATION PROMPTS, AND JOB REPORT LOGO

- Routes style-specific purchasing notes only to the matching product line. For
  example, “980 style instead of 966” now stops only the 966 line instead of
  unrelated hats, shirts, or other products in the same order.
- Shows a visible Instruction Decision control for non-decoration customer notes,
  so style substitutions and similar order-specific changes can be acknowledged
  after the affected fields are corrected.
- In All Orders + Standard Orchid Workflow, blanket embroidery notes such as
  “Embroider everything,” “Embroider all,” and “EMB everything” still create
  Purchase Review prompts for every applicable product line in the order.
- Any note that says screen print, screen-print, screenprint, screen printed, or
  screen printing now remains a Purchase Review prompt in Standard Orchid
  Workflow. Product/style and garment-family targeting still limits the prompt to
  the applicable lines when the note identifies them.
- Restores the Logo button to the left sidebar and allows a job logo to be uploaded
  for standard outsourced screen-print reports as well as Entire Order Outsourced
  packets.
- Includes Professional 4.8.32 and all earlier fixes.
